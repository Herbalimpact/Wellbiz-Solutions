  • Business Planning
    Business planning involves defining a company's goals, products or services, target market, operational structure, marketing approach, and financial requirements. It provides a clear roadmap for starting, managing, and growing the business. This task may include preparing business plans, setting performance targets, identifying required resources, analyzing risks, and establishing practical steps for achieving sustainable profitability and long-term success.
  • Strategic Planning
    Strategic planning focuses on determining the long-term direction of an organization and identifying the actions required to achieve its vision. It includes reviewing the current business position, defining strategic objectives, analyzing internal strengths and weaknesses, assessing external opportunities and threats, and developing measurable initiatives. The process helps management allocate resources effectively, improve competitiveness, and align daily activities with broader organizational priorities.
  • Scenario Planning
    Scenario planning involves exploring different possible future conditions and evaluating how they could affect the organization. This task includes identifying major uncertainties, developing realistic best-case, expected-case, and worst-case scenarios, and assessing the financial and operational impact of each situation. It helps decision-makers prepare flexible response strategies, reduce exposure to unexpected risks, and make informed choices under changing market, economic, regulatory, or competitive conditions.
