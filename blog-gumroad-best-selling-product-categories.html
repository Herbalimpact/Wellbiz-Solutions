I Studied Gumroad's Sales Data: 5 Product Categories Where One Product Can Earn $500 a Month

Everyone says "sell digital products online". I wanted to know which products actually sell. Here is what I found when I looked at the numbers behind 146,271 Gumroad products, and what it means for you if you want to earn from your knowledge.

I sell my own business guides and e-books online, on Payhip and on Gumroad. So when people ask me, "Juma, what should I sell online?", I don't want to answer with a guess. Recently I set myself a simple question: on Gumroad, which kinds of products earn at least $500 a month each?

$500 a month is not a fortune, but it is a serious side income in many countries, and it is the point where a digital product stops being a hobby and starts being a business. So I went looking for real data, not success stories.

Where the numbers come from: the main source is InsightRaider's 2026 study of 146,271 public Gumroad products in 18 categories, with about $206 million in tracked revenue (data window January–April 2026). Revenue is estimated from each product's price and its visible sales signals, not reported by the sellers, so treat the figures as a good guide rather than exact accounts.

First, the Hard Truth About Selling on Gumroad

Before I share the winning categories, I owe you honesty. Gumroad is not a place where everyone makes money. The data shows a market where a few products take almost everything:

  • The median Gumroad creator earns about $72 a month.
  • The top 1% of products take 77.3% of all tracked revenue.
  • 44% of products earned nothing at all.
  • A separate tracker of 3.8 million Gumroad listings found that only about 0.4% of products ever pass $1,000 in total sales.

So a product that earns $500 every month is already in the top few percent. That is the bar. The good news is that the products that clear it are not random. They cluster in a few categories, and their sellers follow a few clear habits.

The 5 Gumroad Categories Where One Product Can Earn $500 a Month

Total sales in a category can mislead you, because a crowded category can have big totals and tiny earnings per seller. So I ranked Gumroad's ten largest categories by average estimated revenue per product. $500 a month is about $6,000 a year; all five of these categories average well above that.

1. Software Development: the giant

This is Gumroad's biggest category by far, with about $65.8 million, nearly a third of all tracked revenue. Buyers here are professionals who pay for anything that saves them hours: a Photoshop script, a design plugin, a coding template. One Photoshop AI script priced at $50 has sold an estimated 11,700 copies. The catch: you need real technical skill to compete here.

2. Fitness & Health: few sellers, strong earnings

Only 379 products, yet about $11,100 per product. People pay for a clear plan from someone they trust: a 12-week programme, a meal plan, a guide to managing a specific problem. Credibility is everything here, so a licensed or experienced practitioner has a real edge. Be careful never to promise cures.

3. Business & Money: the practical choice

About $15.4 million in total and $10,100 per product. Buyers want something that helps them earn or save: a business plan template, a pricing guide, a sales system. One business course priced at about $199 has earned an estimated $1.6 million. This is where most of my own products sit, because people will pay for a tool that pays for itself.

4. Education: one strong guide can carry a business

About $8,700 per product. My favourite example in the whole dataset is an English-speaking pattern guide, a simple PDF priced at $290, with estimated sales of about $568,000. It shows that a single e-book that solves one painful problem very well can outsell entire courses.

5. Self-Improvement: systems, not motivation

About $8,600 per product. The winners here are not inspirational e-books; they are tools people use every day. The best-known example is Ultimate Brain for Notion, a $129 productivity system with an estimated $1.9 million in sales.

Hidden gem: Writing & Publishing. It is not one of the ten largest categories, but it has only 226 products and averages about $15,750 per product, with the highest median sales of any category in the study. Guides on writing, self-publishing and getting published face little competition.

Categories I would avoid as a newcomer: the same study flags Photography, Gaming and Recorded Music as crowded or full of buyers who won't pay much. 3D Assets and Design also earn well per product, but competition there is heavy.

What $500 a Month Really Takes: The Sales Maths

Gumroad has no monthly fee, but it takes 10% + $0.50 on every direct sale, with card processing of about 2.9% + $0.30 on top. When a buyer finds you through Gumroad's own Discover marketplace, the fee is a flat 30%. Here is what that means for $500 a month from direct sales:

6 Things the $500-a-Month Sellers Do Differently

1. They charge properly

The data shows the $30–$49 price range converts best, giving the best balance between the number of buyers and the money per sale. Courses average about $96. Most new sellers underprice and then need impossible sales volumes.

Try this: price by the value of the problem you solve, not by the number of pages.

2. They sell more than one product

Sellers with several products earn about 19 times more than single-product sellers. Every buyer of your first product is a warm prospect for the second. Build a small family of related products: a guide, a template pack, a course.

3. They build their own audience

Gumroad calls itself a marketplace, but it does not bring most buyers to you. Email brings in about 42% of all Gumroad sales. Start an email list or a WhatsApp channel before you launch, and send your own buyers to your direct link, where you pay 10% instead of 30%.

4. They solve one specific, painful problem

"How to speak English with confidence using patterns" sells. "General English tips" does not. The narrower and more urgent the problem, the easier it is to sell.

5. They collect reviews

Ratings and reviews are the social proof that turns a visitor into a buyer. Ask your first customers for an honest review, and make it easy for them.

6. They improve and keep going

Even creators with an audience usually wait 60–90 days for their first $100; without an audience, it often takes 6–12 months. Top earners typically spent one to two years building and improving before reaching big numbers. Treat your first product as version one.

My Advice If You Are Starting Out

  1. Choose from the five strong categories where your real experience lies: health, business, education, self-improvement or software.
  2. Write down one painful problem your audience faces, and build the product that solves it.
  3. Price it between $30 and $49 if it delivers real results.
  4. Start your email or WhatsApp list now, before the product is ready.
  5. Plan your second product from the questions your first buyers ask.
  6. Sell on more than one platform. I use both Gumroad and Payhip, which charges lower fees on its paid plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

What sells best on Gumroad?

By revenue per product, the strongest of Gumroad's larger categories in 2026 are Software Development, Fitness & Health, Business & Money, Education and Self-Improvement. By format, digital downloads and e-books sell the most copies, while courses sell at the highest average price.

How much does the average Gumroad seller earn?

Much less than the success stories suggest. One 2026 analysis of 146,271 products put the median creator at about $72 a month, while the top 1% of products took 77.3% of all tracked revenue. A product earning $500 a month is already in the top few percent.

How many sales do I need to make $500 a month on Gumroad?

On direct sales Gumroad keeps 10% plus $0.50, and card processing of about 2.9% plus $0.30 also applies. After those fees you need roughly 41 sales a month at $15, 20 sales at $30, or 12 sales at $49. Sales through Gumroad's Discover marketplace cost a flat 30%, so you need a few more.

Can I sell on Gumroad without a big audience?

Yes, but Gumroad will not bring most of the buyers for you. Email brings in about 42% of Gumroad sales, so start building an email or WhatsApp list early, pick a narrow problem to solve, and price your product in the $30 to $49 range if it delivers real value.

Final Word

Gumroad rewards a small number of sellers very well and pays most others very little. What separates the two groups is not luck. They chose a category where buyers spend, solved one clear problem, charged a fair price, built their own audience and kept improving. You can do the same with the knowledge you already have.
