Want to Be Wealthy? If You're Employed, Your Wealth-Building Hours Are 3 PM to 10 PM

🇹🇿 Soma kwa Kiswahili

Do you want to be wealthy? It's almost a silly question. Who doesn't? If someone out there truly has no interest in wealth, they're probably one in a million.

Wanting Wealth Is Not the Same as Building It

Yet there's a huge gap between wanting to be rich and actually becoming rich. Nearly all of us want wealth, but the number who actually end up with it may be as few as one in a hundred thousand.

Why? Because most of us haven't accepted a simple truth: nothing is free. Everything has a price — and wealth has a price of its own. If you aren't willing to pay that price, you're better off letting go of the dream altogether.

The Price of Wealth: How You Use Your Time

One of the biggest prices you must pay to become wealthy is using your time well. If you're employed, think about your typical day. From around 6 AM, when you leave home for work, until about 3 PM, when you leave your employer's premises, those hours are already sold. They belong to your employer.

And since most of us are in bed by around 10 PM, that leaves just one window you truly own for building wealth: between 3 PM and 10 PM.

Wealth Is Not Luck — It's What You Do After Work

Understand this clearly: becoming wealthy is not a matter of luck. How you spend those hours between 3 PM and 10 PM is what will decide whether you become wealthy or stay exactly where you are today.

This is the only time you have to invest in learning what you don't yet know about building wealth, such as:

  • How to build a network of people you can do business with
  • How to raise the capital you need to start a business
  • How to protect the capital you already have
  • And much more

Already Running a Side Business? This Is Your Management Time

If you've already managed to start a business alongside your job, these same hours are your only real opportunity to manage it properly — checking the books, following up with customers, restocking and planning the next move. A business left on autopilot rarely grows; a business that gets your evenings can.

Your Next Step

Tonight, before the TV or the phone takes over, ask yourself one question: what did I do between 3 PM and 10 PM that moved me closer to wealth? Your honest answer is your starting point.
